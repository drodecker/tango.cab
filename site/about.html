Corporate Architecture

Child-Partner LLC deployment model

Tango Cab is established under the LLC laws of Texas, with localized child-partner LLCs established for each regional deployment.

Tango.Cab LLC (Texas)

Parent technology entity, platform licensing, telematics coordination, and centralized operational IP.

OC.Cab / OC's Tango (California)

Dedicated child LLC holding vehicle assets and long term commercial property leases in Orange County with government ties.

Tango Nevada LLC (Nevada)

Dedicated entity managing the Las Vegas hub, entertainment corridor, and regional terminal depots.

Tango Florida LLC (Florida)

Regional asset vehicle pool positioned for Tampa, Orlando, and Miami Sunbelt commercial fleet expansion.

🛡️ Ring-Fenced Capital: Each localized fleet deployment (Tango Station, Tango Fleet, or Tango Depot) operates under its own child-partner LLC pool, isolating asset liability, simplifying local property partner distributions, and providing clean pass-through tax efficiency for investors.
